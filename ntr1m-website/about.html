About NTR1M

By Skaters,
For Skaters.

NTR1M Skate Academy was founded in February 2024 with a simple goal: make quality inline skating coaching more accessible and inspire more people to take up skating.

Our Story

The academy comes to you.

Traditional lesson models often require skaters to travel to a fixed training venue. NTR1M takes a different approach: coaching is conducted at suitable locations across Singapore, reducing barriers to getting started while keeping lessons structured and personal.

The academy combines an accessible model with coaching informed by national-team and international competition experience.

Mission

Make quality coaching accessible.

To make quality inline skating coaching accessible to everyone, helping skaters build confidence, develop strong foundations and discover a lasting passion for skating.

Vision

Inspire more people to skate.

To inspire more people to take up inline skating and build a community where quality coaching and meaningful progression are accessible to everyone.

Motto

By Skaters, For Skaters.

An academy shaped by people who understand the sport, the learning process and the experience of being a skater.

Our Values

What we develop beyond technique.

Confidence

Helping skaters trust their ability and approach new challenges positively.

Sportsmanship

Respect for coaches, teammates, competitors and the wider skating community.

Passion

Creating experiences that make skaters want to keep learning and enjoying the sport.

Independence

Developing the control and judgement to skate confidently beyond coached sessions.
