Policies

Simple, Clear
Expectations.

These policies help lessons run consistently for students, parents and coaches.

Policy summary for general reference. Specific arrangements confirmed by NTR1M for your booking take priority.

Cancellations & Rescheduling

Cancellations made less than 4 hours before the lesson, and no-shows, are charged as attended. Cancellations made at least 4 hours before the lesson may be rescheduled. If NTR1M cancels a lesson, it will be rescheduled.

Weather

Where possible, lessons will continue under suitable shelter. If a lesson cannot proceed, the affected lesson will be refunded. If more than 50% of the lesson has already been completed, it is charged as completed.

Group Lessons

There are no individual make-up sessions for missed group lessons. The student simply returns for the next scheduled session.

Late Arrival

If a student arrives late, the original lesson end time remains unchanged. If the coach is late, the lesson will be extended to provide the full booked duration.

Equipment & Safety

Inline skates and a helmet are mandatory during lessons. Wrist, knee and elbow protection are recommended and remain the participant's choice. Students must follow coach instructions and safe-use rules throughout training.

Payments, Packages & Refunds

Paid packages and lesson fees are non-refundable except where NTR1M specifically confirms otherwise, including qualifying weather cancellations. Packages are not transferable between participants.

Photography & Video

Photography or video may be taken during academy activities for coaching, documentation and academy communications, subject to applicable consent and privacy requirements.

Competitions

Competition entries, event fees and related payments become non-refundable once NTR1M has submitted or paid the registration to the organiser, unless the organiser provides a refund.
