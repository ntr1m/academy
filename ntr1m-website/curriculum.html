Structure.

Six levels provide a clear development pathway. There is no fixed completion time — skaters progress at their own pace based on coach discretion and demonstrated competency.

01 · BEGINNERFoundationSafety, balance, falling and recovery.
02 · BEGINNERIntroductoryStanding, gliding, movement and stopping.
03 · BEGINNERBasicTurns, control and fundamental technique.
04 · DEVELOPMENTIntermediateTransitions, speed and stronger technique.
05 · DEVELOPMENTAdvancedPrecision, advanced control and confidence.
06 · ELITEMasterRefinement, independence and mastery.
Warm-up→Review→New Skill→Drills→Application→Recap
How progression works

Development, not deadlines.

Every student learns differently. NTR1M does not publish fixed completion times or use formal pass/fail assessments. Coaches use the curriculum as a structured guide and move students forward when they demonstrate the appropriate control, understanding and independence.

Competitive Development sits alongside the six-level curriculum as a separate pathway for skaters who want to train for competition.
