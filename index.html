NTR1M Skate Academy provides premium private inline skating coaching and customized inline skating classes across Singapore.

COACHING
THAT ADAPTS.

Every student starts from a different point. We look at current ability, confidence, movement quality and goals before deciding what to work on next. A beginner may need more time to become comfortable on skates, while a more experienced skater may need sharper technique and more demanding drills.

Lessons are therefore not built around a fixed number of weeks. Progress depends on the individual, and the next step changes as the student develops. The aim is steady, understandable progress without turning learning into a race.

WHY IT WORKS.

Good coaching is more than demonstrating a movement. We watch how a student skates, explain what needs to change, correct technique when necessary and revisit skills when more practice is needed. Clear instruction keeps students from simply copying a movement without understanding it.

The result is a calm, purposeful lesson where students can build confidence while learning skills they can actually use on skates.

CONFIDENCE.

Our coaching combines the perspective of an athlete with the responsibility of a coach. We understand how technical details affect performance, but we also understand that a student needs explanations, encouragement and time to make those details feel natural.

That balance helps us choose drills that are useful without making lessons unnecessarily complicated. Whether someone is learning their first stop or refining more advanced control, the focus remains on understanding the movement and knowing what to work on next.

Patient coaching

We teach at the student's pace and break skills into manageable steps. If something is not clicking, we change the explanation, slow the drill down or approach the movement from another angle. The goal is to build genuine understanding and confidence rather than rush a student through a checklist.

Engaging lessons

Lessons use practical drills, games and varied activities to keep students moving and involved. Exercises can be adapted to age, ability and confidence, so students can practise the same core skill in different ways without feeling like they are simply repeating a routine.

Competition experience

Our coaching is shaped by real competition experience in Inline Freestyle, including international events and high-level competition. That background gives us practical insight into technique, preparation, pressure and performance, which can be brought into training when a student wants to develop further.

FAQ

FREQUENTLY
ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know before your first lesson.

Do I need skating experience?

No. Complete beginners are welcome. We start with the fundamentals and build from there.

Who can join?

Our lessons are open to all ages and skill levels, from first-time skaters to students working on more advanced skills.

Where are lessons held?

Lessons are conducted across Singapore. The exact location can be discussed when arranging your session.

How much do lessons cost?

Lessons start from $50 per lesson. Final pricing depends on location and timing.

How do I book?

Use the BOOK NOW button or WhatsApp us to check availability and arrange a lesson.

How often should I train?

Weekly practice is recommended for consistent progress, but the schedule can be adjusted around your goals and availability.

What should I bring?

Bring your inline skates, suitable protective equipment and water. Let us know if you need help checking your setup.

What happens if it rains?

If weather affects the session, we may pause and move to shelter. If continuing is not practical, the session can be rescheduled.

What happens if a student is injured?

We carry first aid and will administer it for minor injuries. Paramedics may be called for major injuries.

Do I have to compete?

No. Competition is optional. Students interested in competing can choose the optional, more rigorous pathway.
