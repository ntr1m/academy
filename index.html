FAQ

Quick answers before you enquire.

Do I need skating experience?

No. Complete beginners are welcome, and lessons are available from age 3+.

Where are lessons held?

NTR1M coaches islandwide at suitable skating locations. Share your postal code or area when enquiring and we will discuss the arrangement with you.

Private or group coaching?

Private coaching offers more individual attention. Group coaching is for 2–8 skaters of roughly similar ability. Beginners may be advised to build foundations privately first if no suitable group is available.

How fast will I or my child learn?

Progress differs between skaters. We focus on consistent technique, confidence and independence rather than promising a fixed completion time.

What happens if it rains?

We first try to conduct the lesson under suitable shelter. If the lesson cannot proceed, the applicable weather arrangement will be discussed. A lesson that is more than 50% completed is treated as completed.

Do I need to buy skates?

You will need skates and a helmet for lessons. Rental is available at S$15 per session, and we can also guide you on suitable equipment to purchase.

Do I have to compete?

No. Competition is optional. Skaters who are interested can explore Competitive Development and academy competition opportunities.

What happens after completing the curriculum?

Skaters can continue refining technique, skate more independently, explore advanced goals or pursue competitive development depending on their interests.

LESSON POLICIES

The important things to know.

Kept here on the same page so you do not have to hunt through another menu.

Cancellations

Please cancel at least 4 hours before the lesson to reschedule. Cancellations under 4 hours and no-shows are charged as attended.

Late Arrival

If a student arrives late, the scheduled end time remains. If the coach is late, the lesson end time will be extended accordingly.

Group Lessons

Missed group sessions do not have make-up lessons; students continue at the next scheduled session.

Safety

Inline skates and a helmet are mandatory. Additional protective gear is optional but recommended.
