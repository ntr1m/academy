08 / FAQ

QUESTIONS,
ANSWERED.

Everything you need before your first lesson.

Do I need skating experience?

No. Complete beginners are welcome. We start from the fundamentals and build comfort, confidence and control step by step.

Who can join?

All ages from 3+ and all skill levels are welcome, from first-time skaters to students working on advanced skills.

Where are lessons held?

Lessons are available island-wide across Singapore. Contact us and we will discuss a suitable location and schedule.

How much do lessons cost?

Coaching starts from $50/hr. Final pricing depends on the lesson arrangement, location and timing. Contact us for a personalised plan.

Do I need to buy skates?

Students should have their own skates and protective equipment. We can help you choose suitable equipment and can also arrange purchases through NTR1M.

Do I have to compete?

No. Competition is optional. Students who want to compete can progress through a more performance-focused pathway.

What happens in a first lesson?

For beginners, we first introduce key movements without skates, then teach correct standing, falling and movement techniques. The priority is getting comfortable and confident.

What happens if it rains?

We will work with you to pause, move to a suitable sheltered space where practical, or reschedule when weather makes skating unsuitable.
